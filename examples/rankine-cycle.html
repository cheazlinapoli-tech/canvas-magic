Quick Notes: Rankine Cycle Components

Click on the cards to flip them. Understand the function of the four main components of the Ideal Rankine Cycle before taking the quiz.

1. Pump

(Click to flip)

Pump

Increases the feedwater pressure before it is pumped into the Boiler.

2. Boiler

(Click to flip)

Boiler

Adds heat to the working fluid to convert the liquid into high-pressure steam.

3. Turbine

(Click to flip)

Turbine

The steam expands and rotates the turbine blades, producing shaft work.

4. Condenser

(Click to flip)

Condenser

Rejects heat to the surroundings and condenses the steam back into liquid water.

Ideal Cycle Sequence: Pump → Boiler → Turbine → Condenser → Pump
